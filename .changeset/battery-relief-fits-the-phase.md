A battery that has been relieving the fuse no longer lets go all at once when that would put a phase back over the limit. When a kettle came on while the car charged, the battery discharged to keep the busy phase under the fuse. As soon as the meter showed room, it stopped discharging in one step, and the phase went just over the limit again. Now the battery keeps the part of its discharge that the phase cannot take, counting the car's and its own changes that the meter may not show yet, and lets the rest go as the room shows.
