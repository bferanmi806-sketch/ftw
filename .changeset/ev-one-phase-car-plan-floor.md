A car that charges on one phase of a three-phase charger is planned again once FTW sees it. FTW plans such a car at a third of each three-phase order, but it gave the planner the charger's three-phase minimum, 4.1 kW, with those one-phase levels. The planner dropped every level below that minimum and planned no charging, so the car stopped about five minutes into the session and missed its goal. The planner now gets the charger's three-phase levels divided by three, for example 1.4–3.7 kW. Each level is a third of an order the charger accepts, so the car draws what the plan counts.
