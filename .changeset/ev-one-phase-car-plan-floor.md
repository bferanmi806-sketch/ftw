A car that charges on one phase of a three-phase charger is planned again once FTW sees it. FTW plans such a car at 1.4–3.7 kW and orders three times that, but it gave the planner the charger's three-phase minimum of 4.1 kW with those levels. The planner dropped every level below that minimum and planned no charging, so the car stopped about five minutes into the session and missed its goal. The planner now gets a third of the charger's minimum, which is the car's 6 A on its one phase.
