A car can say it was plugged in here. Vehicle drivers may report when their car's cable went in (`plugged_since_ms`) and how far the car is from the site (`site_distance_m`), and `host.site_location()` gives a vehicle driver the site's position so the car's coordinates stay in the driver. The box names a car by its own word even when the report arrives late, and never names a car that says it is somewhere else.
