With phase mode auto, solar-only charging now runs on three phases when the spare solar is above the three-phase minimum, 6 A on each phase (4,140 W at 230 V). Before, a plan that showed little or no solar could keep the car on one phase all day, even with 9 kW spare. The spare solar measured now counts as part of the forecast, and a one-phase choice for the day gives way once the spare solar is above the minimum again. Each change between one and three phases still holds for at least 30 minutes, and a single high reading does not switch to three phases. A step of 16 A on one phase (3,680 W) no longer counts as a three-phase step, so 3.7 to 4.1 kW of spare solar stays on one phase.
