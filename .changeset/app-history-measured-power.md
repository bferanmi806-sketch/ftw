The app's history for solar, grid and battery now shows the measured power, the same as the box's own charts. Before, it came from the energy counters, which on a Sungrow move in 0.1 kWh steps, so each five minutes read 0 or 1.2 kW while the sun gave 0.4 kW. Home use is unchanged. Five minutes in which a flow went unmeasured for more than a tenth of the time is now a gap for that flow, not a lower value.
