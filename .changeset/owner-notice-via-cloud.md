Pairing the first phone no longer shows a failed notification. FTW Cloud sends the "A new owner" notice to the owners' phones, but the notification history only counted the box's own channels. On a new box those have nobody to send to yet, so the row said "failed: no publisher installed" and the bell showed a red count. The history now counts FTW Cloud's notice and says "sent". When no channel had anyone to send to, the row says "no phone or service to send to" instead of the wrong "no publisher installed".
