The dashboard no longer prints a minus sign on power. Charts label their axes with sizes and name what sits on each side of zero: above it what adds to the grid meter's reading (import, charge, the home, the car), below it what takes away (export, discharge, solar). Grid and battery values say their direction in a word, such as "500 W export" or "1.4 kW discharge", in tooltips, the live strip, targets, driver cards, the per-phase watts under the fuse bars and the plan's safety note. The Overview flow shows the battery's size with "charging" or "discharging" under it.
