The plan now keeps each clock hour under the hourly import limit. The planner used to see only the fuse. With a 4.5 kW limit it could plan the car at 4.8 kW for four hours, import 5.5 kWh in an hour and show a charging goal as met that FTW could not deliver. Each plan now gets every clock hour in the box's local time and what the hour in progress has used, and FTW rejects a plan that imports more in an hour than it may. A planned hour keeps a little under the limit, because the car charges in whole amps and FTW raises it only with a step to spare; the plan then promises about what the car gets. When the limit puts a goal out of reach, the plan says how much is missing. The home battery covers the house so the car gets more of the hour. The bundled Energyplan worker does not take the hours yet: when its plan breaks them, FTW uses its own plan. Where FTW cannot plan the site itself, it keeps the worker's plan, says the plan may break the limit, and still holds each hour to it live. A new limit replans at once.
