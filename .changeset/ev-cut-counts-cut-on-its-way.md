Charging is no longer cut twice when a cloud charger is slow to apply a cut. Easee Cloud takes 30–120 s to apply a new current, but FTW cut again after 20 s, before the first cut could show on the site meter, so a kettle on top could take the car from 10,400 W to 0 W. FTW now lets a cut it has sent cover the overage until the charger can have applied it: the time the driver declares, or 3 minutes. Only what a new load adds is cut. A raise still on its way now counts in a cut, so the phase is not left over the limit when the raise lands. Once a phase's averaged current passes 1.25 times the fuse rating, which a gG fuse carries for an hour, FTW cuts at once whatever is on its way.
